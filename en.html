The Idea

An ancient parable, in a child’s language

King Solomon, the wisest of all people, told of a little city and a great king who came to fight against it. Our Sages and the books of Chassidus explain: the little city is a person, and inside are two kings, the good inclination and the evil inclination, each wanting to rule.

In the Two Kings series the parable becomes a story: a child faces a real choice, the Bad King whispers, the Good King answers, and the child discovers that the decision is in his own hands.

How It All Began

Years of a live stage show

The series grew out of a stage play performed for more than fifteen years in schools, summer camps and community centers. In every show, a child from the audience faced a dilemma, and two actors, the Good King and the Bad King, “battled” inside his heart. Many of the children who saw it told us years later how much it affected them.
